Our story

Ten years of building resilience.

2015

Where we began

Ovanova, Inc. partnered with churches, schools and nonprofits to host online events about energy resilience and independence. For every attendee we donated to their chosen cause, raising over $100K and starting thousands of conversations about local energy.

2021

Installing nationwide, and beyond

We founded Ovanova Solar Corporation, an unlimited general and electrical contractor. We've since delivered private power plants in 27 states, interconnected with over 60 utilities and expanded internationally to Kenya.

2023

Serving rural communities

A program helping small businesses and agricultural producers in underserved rural areas access grants and tax credits with no upfront cost. It produced over 100 applications nationwide, representing more than $100 million in solar and storage microgrid projects.

2025

Innovating for tomorrow

Building microgrids across the country showed us the gaps in hardware, software and secure U.S. supply chains. So we began developing our own technology with top engineering and cybersecurity teams.
