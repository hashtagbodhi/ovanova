Why now

The grid wasn't built for today's weather, loads or outages. For families working from home, storing food, using medical equipment or living rurally, every outage is disruptive and costly. A home microgrid gives you control instead of dependency.

How it works

Generate. Store. Control.

Generate

Solar panels create clean, affordable electricity for your home.

Store

Battery storage keeps that energy available day and night, and during outages.

Control

Smart controls manage your home's power automatically and keep everything running when the grid goes down.

Inside the system

Solar, storage and smart controls.

Reliable power when the grid goes down, or whenever you choose to be independent from it.

Load management

Prioritize key appliances, extend battery life and avoid overloading your system, without resetting or watching circuits yourself.

A secure app

Monitor performance, battery levels and energy use from anywhere. At home or away, you stay informed and in control.
