Security

Researched, not assumed.

Before designing the NERD’s security, Ovanova commissioned BC Security to investigate cybersecurity risks in existing solar and energy equipment.

The findings shaped how the NERD handles updates, remote access and local control.

The research behind the design
7CVEs

Cybersecurity vulnerabilities in solar and energy equipment reported by Ovanova and BC Security, and credited by CISA.

The work was presented at DEF CON 33. It is architecture research, not certification of the finished NERD.
