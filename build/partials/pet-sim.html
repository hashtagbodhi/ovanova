      How this works: the same loads, unit specs and 4.5 sun-hours a day as the sizer, in 15-minute steps from 6 pm. Loads run at their average draw during operating hours. If start-up surges exceed a unit's output, the lowest-priority loads stay off. The generator (9 kW on Ember, 26 kW on Flame) starts at 20% charge and stops at 90%. Blaze's solar input is not listed in the line-up, so it runs on its battery alone here.
