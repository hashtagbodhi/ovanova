The problem

The grid is getting harder to manage. Aging infrastructure, variable renewables, rising cyber threats and unpredictable demand cost the U.S. over $125 billion a year in outages and inefficiencies. Distributed energy is growing fast, but without one intelligence layer those assets stay fragmented and underused.

What it does

Forecast. Orchestrate. Protect.

Cloud forecasting, edge intelligence, encrypted field communications and fleet orchestration, so performance stays predictable even when the grid is unstable or communication is limited.

Precision forecasting

Machine learning predicts solar output, consumption and battery availability at every site, from real-time weather, history and local behavior. Stronger day-ahead commitments, fewer imbalance penalties.

Fleet intelligence

Distributed sites act as one utility-grade fleet. When energy is needed, each site gets tailored instructions based on its own solar, load and storage.

Cybersecurity by design

Zero-trust architecture, encrypted LoRaWAN links, inline intrusion detection and a DMZ sandbox that isolates insecure vendor networks from critical infrastructure.

Adaptive integration

One platform for fixed microgrids, distributed DER sites and Ovanova's mobile PET units, with the same intelligent control everywhere.

How a dispatch works

Exactly what's needed, exactly when.

01

Forecast every site

An AI engine predicts solar, consumption and battery availability site by site, turning uncertainty into confidence.

02

Plan continuously

A Model Predictive Control engine re-optimizes schedules as forecasts, constraints and conditions change.

03

Dispatch by consensus

When a utility requests energy, the fleet agrees on each site's share, with no single point of failure.

04

Deliver at the edge

Local edge controllers carry out their instructions, so the fleet delivers on time and on target.
