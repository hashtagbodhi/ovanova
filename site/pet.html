      For owners

      Three ways a PET pays you back.

      Storm-proof resilience

      Tow into outage zones to power clinics, shelters and homes on arrival. Onboard batteries, solar awnings, a bi-fuel generator (propane and natural gas) and rapid EV energy transfer keep the lights on.

      Dock and deliver

      Plug directly into a building for peak shaving, load shifting or backup power.

      Rental income

      PETs rent for $300 to $450 a day. With two busy 100-day seasons, a PET can pay itself off and keep earning after that.
