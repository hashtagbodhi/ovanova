Why local power

The grid was built for another century. Aging lines, rising outages and long-distance losses expose a system that can no longer guarantee power. So we put the power plant next to the people who use it.

DistanceCentral grid: power crosses long lines and loses energy on the way.Generated and stored where it's used.
FailureCentral grid: one fault upstream reaches everyone downstream.Your system keeps running when the grid isn't.
ResponseCentral grid: slow to adapt to changing demand.Solar, storage and grid balanced in real time.
