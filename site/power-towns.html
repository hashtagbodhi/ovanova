      Strategies

      High-value strategies, delivered fast.

      In a few focused sessions, we help your team find where meaningful improvements can be made.

      Peak shaving

      We analyze your load profile to find high-demand periods and reduce strain on your infrastructure. Lower costs, better efficiency, fewer reactive investments.

      Load shifting

      Changing when energy is used, not just how much, makes smarter use of existing capacity, improves stability and opens new value streams.

      Infrastructure deferral

      Instead of defaulting to capital expansion, targeted resilience investments can reduce or delay large upgrades.

      EV and V2G integration

      Electric vehicles and bidirectional charging can serve your grid as well as your customers, turning load into value.

      Reliability metric analysis

      Where applicable, we review CAIDI, SAIDI and SAIFI and turn them into clear, board-ready recommendations.

      With you, not for you
